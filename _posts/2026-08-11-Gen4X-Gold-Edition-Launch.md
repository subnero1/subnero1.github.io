## FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

### Subnero Launches Gen4X Gold Edition: High-Compute Underwater Acoustic Smart Modems

__Singapore, 11 August 2026__ – Subnero, a leader in underwater wireless communication technologies, today announced the launch of the **Gold Edition** of its Gen4X series acoustic smart modems. The Gold Edition is the new premium tier of the Gen4X lineup, engineered for demanding deployments that require enhanced compute power and multi-receiver capability.

While the Silver Edition remains the reliable workhorse of the Gen4X series, the Gold Edition is the high-performance powerhouse for multi-receiver and compute-heavy work. Built on the same proven acoustic foundation, it adds significantly greater onboard processing capability and scales up to 4 receive channels, enabling advanced array processing, spatial diversity, 3D localization, and high-fidelity acoustic sensing.

The Gold Edition also supports an optional **NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano co-processor**, bringing GPU-accelerated AI and deep learning capabilities directly into the modem. This allows users to run inference, edge analytics, and custom algorithms on the modem itself, without any external compute hardware. For data-intensive missions such as passive acoustic monitoring, the Gold Edition ships with 256 GB of onboard storage, with a 1 TB upgrade available.

The Gold Edition is available across all three Gen4X frequency bands, in both standalone and open configurations:

- [L12L Series (LF band)]({{site.baseurl}}/products/wnc/gen4x/l12l/g4x-l12l-gold.html) for long-range communication beyond 10 km
- [M25M Series (MF band)]({{site.baseurl}}/products/wnc/gen4x/m25m/g4x-m25m-gold.html) for versatile general-purpose deployments
- [S40H Series (HF band)]({{site.baseurl}}/products/wnc/gen4x/s40h/g4x-s40h-gold.html) for high-speed links up to 33 kbps

Like every Subnero modem, the Gold Edition runs **UnetStack**, the powerful software-defined underwater networking stack, giving users full programmability over waveforms, protocols, and networked behaviors.

The Gen4X Gold Edition is available to order now. For more information, or to request a demo or quotation, please contact sales@subnero.com or visit www.subnero.com.
