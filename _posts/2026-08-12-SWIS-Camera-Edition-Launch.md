## FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

### Subnero Launches SWIS - Camera Edition: Wireless Imaging from the Seabed, with ML at the Edge

__Singapore, 12 August 2026__ – Subnero, a leader in underwater wireless communication technologies, today announced the launch of [**SWIS - Camera Edition**]({{site.baseurl}}/products/swis/swis-camera.html), the newest member of the Subnero Wireless Integrated Suite (SWIS). SWIS - Camera Edition delivers wireless image transfer from underwater cameras over high-speed acoustic links, bringing on-demand visual monitoring to seabed infrastructure, moorings, and long-term deployments without cables, divers, or recovery operations.

The launch follows yesterday's announcement of the [Gen4X Gold Edition]({{site.baseurl}}/product-launch/press/2026/08/11/Gen4X-Gold-Edition-Launch.html), and the two are closely connected. SWIS - Camera Edition is built on the [S40H Series Gold Edition]({{site.baseurl}}/products/wnc/gen4x/s40h/g4x-s40h-gold.html), whose enhanced onboard compute makes it possible to run machine learning models directly on the modem. Images are analyzed as they are captured, so only the frames that matter are selected, compressed, and transmitted, making the most of the available acoustic bandwidth. The Gold Edition's multi-receiver support further improves link performance for even more reliable image delivery in challenging conditions.

Key capabilities of SWIS - Camera Edition include:

- On-demand and scheduled wireless image retrieval over high-speed acoustic links
- Machine learning processing at the edge to select and compress imagery before transmission
- Low-bandwidth smart previews before committing to full-resolution downloads
- Remote reconfiguration of camera and communication parameters during deployment
- An intuitive web-based interface powered by UnetStack, with no software installation required

SWIS - Camera Edition joins [SWIS - ADCP Edition]({{site.baseurl}}/products/swis/swis-adcp.html) in the growing SWIS family, which turns seabed sensors and cameras into connected, remotely managed observation networks.

SWIS - Camera Edition is available now, and Subnero is welcoming pilot and demo opportunities with early customers and partners. To explore a pilot deployment, request a demo, or discuss how wireless underwater imaging fits your operations, reach out to sales@subnero.com or visit www.subnero.com.
