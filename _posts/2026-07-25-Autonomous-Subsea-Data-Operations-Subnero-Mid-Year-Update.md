Picture this: a researcher wants four months of seabed ADCP data. No divers, no cables, no vessel, just a login from their desk and a download in about two clicks. That is the seabed to shore delivery we have been building toward, and this update rounds up where things stand at the mid-year mark.

### In Focus: Autonomous Data Operations with USVs

Our Acoustic Data Harvesting solution turns uncrewed surface vessels into data mules for the seabed. A USV surveys past a lander, wakes it acoustically, and pulls the data on the move, so the lander stays deployed and the vessel never has to stop and hold station: drive by harvesting instead of a vessel parked overhead, real cost savings compared with a crewed survey, and data delivered to shore in near real time.

### Case Study: From Seabed to Shore at MESN

At the MESN-UBN buoy off Pulau Ubin, four months of seabed current data came home with no divers, no cables, and no vessel deployment, retrieved on demand over an acoustic and cellular link.

> "Being able to download data from the office whenever we want makes our customers' work much easier," said Veronica Tan, Subsea IoT Engineer at Subnero.
