Subnero modems are available in two configurations, each suited for specific use cases:
- **Standalone Configuration (SC)**: Pressure-housed and ideal for surface or seabed deployments.
- **Open Configuration (OC)**: Compact and designed for seamless integration into platforms like AUVs.

Beyond these, [additional receiving channels](https://subnero.com/products/wnc/gen4x/accessories/multi-receiver.html) are available as an optional upgrade, equipping the modem with multiple receiving channels for enhanced communication performance.

For more information about the different configurations, visit: [https://subnero.com/products/#smart-modems](https://subnero.com/products/#smart-modems)
