Subnero modems are available in three editions: Silver, Gold, and Bronze. Silver is the all-round commercial, research, and defense option, and Gold is the higher performance variant with greater compute capacity and support for up to four receive channels. Bronze is a cost optimized platform for early stage testing and education. MIL-qualified builds for demanding operational environments are available on request. Contact us to discuss your requirements.

For more information about the different editions, visit: [https://subnero.com/products/#smart-modems](https://subnero.com/products/#smart-modems)
