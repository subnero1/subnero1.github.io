<div class="row row-width row-padding-bottom">
  <div class="large-6 columns flex-col">
    <p>Subnero Wireless Integrated Suite (SWIS), Camera Edition provides a seamless end-to-end solution for retrieving images from underwater cameras without cables or recovery operations. Built on Subnero's high-speed S40H series acoustic smart modems, it brings on-demand visual monitoring to seabed infrastructure, moorings, and long-term deployments.</p>
    <p>Acoustic bandwidth is precious. SWIS - Camera Edition makes the most of it by processing imagery at the edge: machine learning models running on the modem analyze images as they are captured, so only the frames that matter are selected, compressed, and transmitted. The result is near real-time visual insight from the seabed, over a wireless acoustic link.</p>
