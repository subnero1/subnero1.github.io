        <h1>Remote Transducer</h1>
        <p>The remote transducer option separates the transducer from the modem's electronics housing, connecting the two with a flexible cable. This allows the transducer to be positioned for the best acoustic performance while the electronics housing is mounted where space allows, making it ideal for platforms with tight envelopes or free-flooded sections, such as AUVs, ROVs, and buoys.</p>
        <p>The remote transducer option is available on all standalone configuration (SC) builds, in both Silver and Gold editions, across all frequency bands.</p>
